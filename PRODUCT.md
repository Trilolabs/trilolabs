## Platform

web

## Users

Primary buyers are founders who need AI agents, automation, or AI-backed product work but do not understand the technical stack behind them. They hire Trilolabs to cut through jargon, decide what is worth building, and get working systems without running a bureaucratic process.

Secondary audiences (operators, compliance, healthcare, BFSI) appear through delivered work, but the site’s primary conversation is with the non-technical founder.

## Product Purpose

Trilolabs is an AI automation and product studio. It finds where AI actually fits a business, builds the systems (and sometimes the full product), launches them without disrupting day-to-day operations, and keeps refining them.

Success looks like: a founder who was stuck on “where do we even start with AI?” ends in a one-to-one, result-driven engagement with shipped automation or product — not a slide deck of options.

Primary conversion path on the marketing site: Book a Demo / Book a call (`/book-a-call`), plus direct mail to `info@trilolabs.com`.

## Positioning

No-bullshit, non-bureaucratic, result-driven, one-on-one delivery for founders who are clueless about the tech behind AI agents and related systems. Neighbors sell complexity, process theater, or generic “AI agency” packages; Trilolabs sells clarity and owned outcomes — including full AI + product ownership when that is the job (e.g. Neurasix).

## Product Principles

1. **Founder-first clarity** — Assume the buyer is smart about their business and lost about AI internals; explain and decide, don’t overwhelm.
2. **Results over process** — Prefer one-to-one, outcome-owned engagement over bureaucracy, frameworks-for-show, or agency theater.
3. **Own the outcome** — When the job requires it, own the full stack and product, not just a slide or a pilot.
4. **Honest evidence** — Only claim what can be shown (Humana / Neurasix / Outroom and verified numbers); strip or replace placeholder metrics rather than decorate with fiction.
5. **Identity is sacred** — Keep legal entity visible; keep the blue · ash · white / tripundra commitment; no leftover template brands or fake social proof.
